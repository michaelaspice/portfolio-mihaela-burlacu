# MorpheusGroup — Customer Health & Churn Analysis

## Core conclusion

The analysis identifies unresolved support issues as the strongest operational churn indicator, followed by low feature adoption and low seat utilization.

Churn risk appears particularly high when these factors occur together, suggesting that customer health should be evaluated through a combination of product engagement and unresolved service friction rather than any single metric.

## Strongest signals

1. **Unresolved tickets**
2. **Feature adoption**
3. **Seat utilization**

## Highest-risk profile

**Low adoption + Low seat utilization + Multiple unresolved tickets**

- Customers: 23
- Churn rate: 60.9%

## Useful negative findings

Account age and renewal timing did not provide meaningful standalone separation in this dataset. Customer sentiment and CSAT were also comparatively weak/noisy.

## Important limitation

The analysis identifies association, not causation.
