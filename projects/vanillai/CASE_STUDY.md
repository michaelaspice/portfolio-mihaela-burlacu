# Elix.ai — Revenue Operations Funnel & ICP Analysis

## The problem
Elix.ai generated 1,250 leads across multiple regions, industries, and acquisition channels. The goal was to understand where pipeline value was being lost and identify which customer segments and acquisition channels deserved greater investment.

## Analytical path
1. Established the overall funnel baseline.
2. Compared lead-source quality and downstream conversion.
3. Added won ARR and ARR-per-lead to separate volume from commercial efficiency.
4. Investigated Outbound by company size and enterprise loss reasons.
5. Compared company-size economics across the full business.
6. Tested industry performance and deprioritized Healthcare as a primary acquisition focus.
7. Compared regions and identified DACH, Nordics, and Benelux as the strongest markets.
8. Combined industry, company size, and region into an ICP hypothesis.
9. Validated the ICP against all other leads at every funnel stage.
10. Compared acquisition channels within the ICP.

## Core findings
- Overall lead-to-win conversion: **8.4%**
- Target ICP: **SaaS & Fintech + 501+ employees + DACH/Nordics/Benelux**
- ICP lead-to-win: **20.0% vs 7.9%**
- ICP ARR per lead: **€13,680 vs €2,663**
- ICP average won deal: **€68.4k vs €33.6k**
- ICP generated **€684k**, about **18% of total won ARR**, from only **4% of leads**
- Referral, Partners, and Organic Search were the strongest-performing acquisition sources within the ICP
- Outbound improved materially when focused on the ICP
- Healthcare showed the weakest commercial efficiency and underperformed through the funnel

## GTM recommendations
1. Prioritize acquisition around the identified ICP.
2. Increase focus on Referral, Partners, and Organic Search.
3. Keep Outbound, but make targeting more ICP-specific.
4. Review LinkedIn and Events before further investment.
5. Deprioritize Healthcare as a primary acquisition segment.
6. Reduce emphasis on small-company and lower-value regional segments.
7. Investigate enterprise pricing and positioning, without changing pricing based on a tiny sample.
8. Continue validating the ICP as lead volume grows.

## Limitations
The dataset is synthetic. The ICP sample includes only 50 leads and 10 wins, so findings should guide testing and resource allocation rather than be treated as causal proof or permanent market truth.
