Operating architecture

Capture once. Structure at source. Pull only what is needed. Communicate information separately from attention.
01 · Source systems

Zendesk · Aircall · HubSpot · Product usage · Typeform · Notion · Deputy. Structured fields are captured where the work happens.

02 · Aurority layer

Triggers · evidence · Quality Issue ID · owner · interdepartmental requests · response status · actions · rechecks.

03 · Action surfaces

Quality feeds hold detailed notifications. Normal department channels receive only attention nudges when thresholds require action.
