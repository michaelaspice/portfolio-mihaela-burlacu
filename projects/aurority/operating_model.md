# Operating Model

## Interdepartmental requests
Requests stay attached to the same Quality Issue ID. The receiving department owes a response, not necessarily an immediate fix.

## Ownership
Handoff does not equal abandonment. A technical issue can have Product as resolution owner while Support remains customer-communication owner.

## Response expectation
The internal SLA measures acknowledgement/response so customer-facing teams are never left without an answer.

## Recheck
Typical windows: 7, 14 and 30 days. Success means the triggering customer or business outcome improves, not merely that an action was marked complete.

## Complexity rule
Escalation depth should be proportional to problem complexity. A broken field should not require a steering committee.
